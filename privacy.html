1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

When you create a SaveCode account or use our services, we collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • First and last name
  • Email address
  • Password (stored in encrypted form — we never store plain-text passwords)
  • Physical address (submitted for verification purposes)
  • Names and relationships of authorized household members you add to your account
  • Communications you send to us, including support requests and partnership inquiries

Information Generated by Our System

As part of the address verification process, our system generates:

  • A unique Home ID associated with your verified address
  • A verification PIN sent to your physical address by mail
  • Records of address verification queries made by authorized parties
  • Account activity logs

Automatically Collected Information

When you use our website, we may automatically collect certain technical information, including:

  • IP address and browser type
  • Pages visited and time spent on our website
  • Referring website or source

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To create and manage your SaveCode account
  • To verify your physical address through our mail-based verification process
  • To generate and maintain your Home ID
  • To respond to verification queries from parties you have authorized
  • To send you account notifications, security alerts, and activity updates
  • To respond to your support requests and inquiries
  • To improve our services and develop new features
  • To comply with applicable laws and legal obligations

🔒 SaveCode does not use your personal information for advertising purposes and does not sell your data to third parties.

3. Information Sharing

Authorized Parties

When an authorized party (such as a financial institution, delivery service, or agency you have authorized) queries your address through the SaveCode network, we provide only your verified address status — we do not share your name, email address, or other personal details unless explicitly required and authorized.

Service Providers

We work with trusted third-party service providers to operate our platform, including:

  • Supabase — database and authentication services
  • Vercel — website hosting
  • Lob.com — physical mail delivery for verification letters
  • Stripe — payment processing

These providers are bound by contractual obligations to protect your information and may only use it to provide services on our behalf.

Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required to do so by law, court order, or governmental authority, or when we believe disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, your safety, or the safety of others.

We Do Not Sell Your Data

SaveCode does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party for marketing or commercial purposes.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. These measures include:

  • Encryption of passwords and sensitive data
  • Encrypted Home IDs that cannot be reverse-engineered to reveal personal information
  • Secure HTTPS connections for all data transmission
  • Row-level security controls on our database
  • Access controls limiting who can access user data within our organization

⚠️ No method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

Access and Correction

You may access and update your personal information at any time through your SaveCode account dashboard.

Deletion

You may request deletion of your account and associated personal information by contacting us at privacy@savecode.us or through your account settings. Note that certain information may be retained as required by law or for legitimate business purposes.

Authorized Party Control

You may add or remove authorized parties at any time through your account dashboard, controlling who can query your address verification status.

Opt-Out of Notifications

You may manage your notification preferences through your account settings at any time.

California Residents

If you are a California resident, you may have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). Please contact us at privacy@savecode.us to exercise these rights.

6. Cookies and Tracking

SaveCode uses essential cookies necessary for the operation of our platform, including session management and security. We do not use third-party advertising cookies or tracking technologies for marketing purposes.

You may disable cookies through your browser settings, though this may affect the functionality of our services.

7. Children's Privacy

SaveCode is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete that information promptly.

If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to SaveCode, please contact us at privacy@savecode.us.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will notify you by email or by posting a prominent notice on our website. The date at the top of this policy indicates when it was last updated.

Your continued use of SaveCode after any changes to this Privacy Policy constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
