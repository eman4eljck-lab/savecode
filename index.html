How It Works

Four Steps to a Verified Address Identity

STEP 01
📋
Register Your Address
Residents register their physical address and all household members — including children — in the SaveCode Resident Registry.
STEP 02
🔑
Receive Your Home ID
A unique Home Identification Code is assigned — verified via physical mail, creating a trusted, persistent address-identity link.
STEP 03
🔍
Institutions Verify
Banks, delivery services, and authorized agencies query the SaveCode API to verify address-person associations in real time.
STEP 04
🛡️
Risk Indicator Raised
If a non-registered individual uses your address, SaveCode generates a risk indicator and notifies relevant parties immediately.
