Pilot Program

Help Test Safer, Simpler Delivery Verification

SaveCode is inviting a limited number of households and delivery partners to test a privacy-first way to confirm whether a recipient is authorized at a verified address.

Join the Pilot For Delivery Partners

How the Pilot Works

Step 1

Register Your Address

Create a SaveCode account and register your home address.

Step 2

Add Authorized Recipients

Add household members you want to authorize to receive deliveries at your address.

Step 3

Simple Driver Check

A participating driver scans the SaveCode QR code or enters the Home ID before handoff.

What the Driver Sees

To protect household privacy, the driver receives only a simple verification result.

VERIFIED ✓ The recipient is authorized for this address.
NOT AUTHORIZED ✕ The recipient is not authorized for this address.

Privacy-first: The driver does not need to see household phone numbers, email addresses, or private account information as part of this verification result.

What We Want to Learn

  • How quickly drivers can complete the verification step

  • Whether it can help reduce misdeliveries or recipient confusion
