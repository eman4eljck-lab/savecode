The Process

How SaveCode Protects Your Home

From registration to real-time fraud detection — here's exactly how SaveCode's verified address identity system works.

Step by Step

The Verification Process

1

Register Your Address & Household

Create your SaveCode account and register your physical address. Add all household members — spouse, children, parents, anyone who lives at the address. Even newborns can be added to protect their identity from day one.

Why this matters: Children's Social Security numbers are prime targets for identity thieves. By registering them in the Resident Registry, any attempt to use their identity at a different address will generate an immediate risk indicator.
2

Physical Address Verification via Mail

SaveCode sends a verification letter to your physical address containing your unique Home ID code. You enter this code in the app to confirm that you are a legitimate resident of the registered address.

Why physical mail? This creates an unforgeable link between the digital identity and the physical location. Anyone can claim an address online — but only a legitimate resident can receive mail at that address.
3

Your Home ID Is Activated

Once verified, your address receives an active Home ID — a unique identifier persistently associated with your address and updatable under authorized conditions such as security events or fraud detection. Your Home ID is the foundation of your address identity.

How it works: The Home ID is linked to your Resident Registry. Financial institutions, delivery services, and authorized agencies can query the SaveCode API to verify if a specific person is a registered resident of a specific address — in real time.
4

Continuous Protection — Active 24/7

From the moment your Home ID is active, SaveCode monitors for unauthorized use of your address. Any attempt to use your address by a non-registered individual triggers an immediate risk indicator to the requesting institution and an alert to you.

What you receive: Real-time notifications when your address is queried, with full details of the requesting institution and the nature of the request.
