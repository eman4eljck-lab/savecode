Our Story

Building America's Address
Identity Infrastructure

SaveCode was founded with a simple belief: every home deserves a verified, protected identity in the digital world.

Our Mission

Every year, billions of dollars are lost to package theft, address fraud, and identity theft — much of it enabled by the lack of a trusted, verified address identity system in the United States.

SaveCode exists to change that. We are building a physical-digital verification infrastructure that gives every home a secure, encrypted identity — protecting residents, enabling trusted commerce, and helping institutions verify addresses with confidence.

Our patent-pending technology bridges the physical and digital worlds through a process that is significantly harder to fake remotely than digital-only methods: physical mail delivery to the address itself.

Founded By

Eman Eljack

Founder & Inventor · Mangel Technologies LLC

Eman Eljack is the founder and sole inventor of SaveCode, operating under Mangel Technologies LLC, based in Everett, Washington. She identified the critical gap in America's address verification infrastructure and developed the patent-pending Home ID system to address it.

SaveCode is built on the belief that physical address identity should be as secure and verifiable as any other form of identity — and that the solution must be grounded in the physical world, not just the digital one.

Our Principles

The values that guide every decision we make at SaveCode.

🔒

Privacy First

SaveCode is designed to share only what is necessary — your verified address status — without exposing personal identity details to third parties.

🏠

Physical Grounding

Our verification is anchored in the physical world. A code sent to your home by mail is something that is significantly harder to forge remotely than digital-only methods — that's our foundation.

⚖️

Transparency

We are clear about what SaveCode does and does not do. We generate indicators and notifications — we do not make final decisions on behalf of any party.

🤝

Trust Through Action

Trust is not claimed — it is earned. SaveCode builds trust through its verification process, not through marketing language alone.

🔬

Innovation

SaveCode's patent-pending technology represents a new approach to address identity — one that bridges the physical and digital in a way not done before.

🌍

Accessibility

Address security should not be a luxury. SaveCode is designed to be accessible to all residents — homeowners and renters alike.
