The Process

How SaveCode Works

A simple, secure three-step process that creates a verified identity for your physical address — backed by patent-pending technology.

For Homeowners & Renters

Getting your Home ID is straightforward. Here's exactly what happens from registration to full protection.

1

Create Your Account

Register on SaveCode with your email address and the physical address you want to verify. Your account is created immediately — but full access is pending address verification.

📧
Email RegistrationProvide a valid email address to create your SaveCode account.
🏠
Address SubmissionEnter the physical address you wish to register and verify.
💳
One-Time Verification FeeA small one-time fee covers the cost of physical mail verification.
⏳
Account PendingYour account is created and awaits address verification to become fully active.
2

Receive Your Verification Letter

SaveCode sends a physical letter to your registered address via mail. The letter contains a unique 6-digit verification code. This step confirms that you have physical access to the address — something that cannot be faked digitally.

✉️
Physical Mail DeliveryA letter containing your unique 6-digit code is mailed to your address.
🔢
Unique 6-Digit CodeEach code is unique to your address and account — it cannot be reused or transferred.
📅
Delivery WindowAllow standard postal delivery time for your letter to arrive.
🔒
Code ExpiresFor security, codes have a limited validity window after issuance.
3

Enter Your Code & Activate Your Home ID

Log in to your SaveCode account and enter the 6-digit code from your letter. Once confirmed, your Home ID is activated — your address is now verified and protected.

✅
Code VerificationEnter your code in your account dashboard to confirm physical access.
🆔
Home ID ActivatedYour unique encrypted Home ID is generated and tied to your verified address.
👨‍👩‍👧
Add Family MembersAuthorize additional household members under your Home ID.
🛡️
Protection ActiveYour address is now part of the SaveCode verified network.
4

Stay Protected

Your Home ID works quietly in the background. Delivery services, financial institutions, and authorized parties can verify your address through SaveCode — while you receive notifications of any queries made against your address.

🔔
Activity NotificationsStay informed when your address is queried through the SaveCode network.
🔄
Address UpdatesMoving? You can update your address — subject to a new verification process.
🔌
Works with PartnersSaveCode integrates with delivery and financial services via our Open API.
📱
Manage Your AccountView and manage your Home ID, household members, and settings anytime.

🛡️ SaveCode's physical mail step is the core of our verification system. Unlike digital-only methods, physical mail delivery confirms that a person has actual access to an address — a standard that is significantly harder to bypass remotely than digital-only verification. Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about how SaveCode works.

How long does verification take? ▼
Verification depends on standard postal delivery times to your address. Once you receive your letter and enter the code, your Home ID is activated immediately.
What if I don't receive my letter? ▼
If your letter does not arrive within a reasonable time, you can request a new code through your account dashboard. Please allow full postal delivery time before requesting a replacement.
Can I register more than one address? ▼
Each SaveCode account supports one primary verified address. If you need to verify a different address, a new verification process is required. Additional address registration options may be available in the future.
Who can see my verified address status? ▼
Only parties with authorized access to the SaveCode network can query your address verification status. SaveCode is designed to share only your verified status — not your personal identity details — with authorized parties, subject to our Privacy Policy and applicable law.
What happens when I move? ▼
You can update your address through your account. A new physical mail verification will be required for your new address to maintain the integrity of the verification system.
Is SaveCode available everywhere in the US? ▼
SaveCode currently supports US addresses. Registration is open now — create your account, submit your address, and you'll receive a verification PIN by mail within 5-7 business days to confirm it.
