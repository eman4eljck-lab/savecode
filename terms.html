5. Address Verification Process

Registration and PIN

When you register a physical address, SaveCode will mail a unique verification PIN to that address. You must have physical access to the address to receive and enter the PIN. This process is designed to confirm genuine residency.

Accuracy of Information

You represent and warrant that the address you register is your current, legitimate physical address and that you have the right to register it. Registering an address that does not belong to you or that you do not reside at is a violation of these Terms and may constitute fraud.

One Address Per Account

Each SaveCode account may register one primary verified address at a time. Address updates require a new verification process.

Fraud Prevention

SaveCode actively monitors for fraudulent registration attempts. If another account attempts to register your verified address, the current registered account holder will be notified. Fraudulent registration attempts will be blocked and reported.
